A powerful and open-source Android application that allows you to easily download videos from your favorite social media platforms (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok) directly to your device's gallery. Written natively in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose for a clean Material 3 UI, it uses the embedded Python engine via Chaquopy and yt-dlp under the hood. Supports sharing integration, high-quality downloads, and dark theme. No telemetry, ads, or tracking.
